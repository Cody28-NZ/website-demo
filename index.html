About Khong Thai Kitchen

A relaxed Thai restaurant with fresh wok flavour.

Khong Thai Kitchen is built around the kind of Thai food people come back for: hot curries, glossy noodles, bright basil, generous portions and service that feels easy from the moment you arrive.

The restaurant is casual, comfortable and family friendly, with dine in, takeaway and delivery available from Albany on Auckland’s North Shore.

Please note: Let the team know about allergies before ordering. The restaurant notes it cannot guarantee food is completely allergen or gluten free due to the risk of cross-contamination in the kitchen.
